Naruto Mobile
Progression & Economy Systems

Using the MDA framework, the systems are split into mechanics, dynamics and aesthetics, and every conclusion is written as a chain: which rule → what players do because of it → what they feel. Yiren Zhi Xia, from the same studio group, serves as the A/B comparison. Based on the live version of October 2026 and a V12 account.

01

Summary

Naruto Mobile makes ninjas its single core product and sells them to skill-focused and progression-focused players at the same time through two opposite PvP rule sets: the Arena normalizes stats, while Ranked Points matches settle by power rating.

LayerApproachKey evidence
Mechanics3 currency tiers, 8 recruit entry points, 7 progression systems; the Arena normalizes power, Ranked Points matches settle by powerPremium Recruit gives S-rank shards at 11.5%, with one guaranteed per ten-pull; every V12 perk is about extra attempts
DynamicsArena players spend in bursts around new ninjas, power players spend daily on stamina; level caps gate time, attempt caps gate output, and money can only loosen the attempt capsRaid levels 110–160 in 5 tiers; the magic-resist threshold for Secret Realm accessories rises about 10% per tier

02

A/B with Yiren Zhi Xia

Both games come from Tencent's Magic Cube studio group and are anime-IP fighting games, but their monetization goes in opposite directions.

  • What is soldOwnership and quantityNaruto sells ninjas, scrolls, summons and power; Yiren Zhi Xia publicly states that characters are free through log-in rewards and quests, and monetizes mainly through skins.
  • How fairness worksPartly shielded / fully shieldedNaruto shields stats in some modes and uses ban slots to make a deep ninja roster valuable again; Yiren Zhi Xia shields level and gear across all of PvP.
  • RisksOpposite directionsNaruto risks power creep and an ever-deeper power line; Yiren Zhi Xia risks a low ceiling on cosmetic spending, and breaking its fairness promise once progression enters PvP.
  • MethodOne chain per conclusionTo keep MDA from turning into labeling, every conclusion runs from rule to player behavior to emotion, backed by in-game screenshots as evidence.

03

Strengths, issues and proposals

  • Three strengths
    1. Each ninja serves both progression lines, so one release sells to two kinds of players, giving revenue both peaks and a stable base.
    2. Anti-frustration rules sit in the low and middle ranks: no points lost on a defeat at low rank, and a rank floor in the middle.
    3. Level gates advance only with time and money can only add attempts, which keeps the rate of content consumption under control.
  • Three issues
    1. Free players can never get coupon-channel ninjas, and have no chance to practice against them while they are strong.
    2. Premium Recruit only discloses the guaranteed S-rank shard per ten-pull, not how many pulls it takes to max an S-rank ninja.
    3. The power line already has 7 progression systems, so the catch-up cost for returning and new players rises every year.
  • Three proposals
    1. Show pity progress for Premium Recruit, consistent with the luck meter already used in Wish Treasure.
    2. Four weeks after a paid ninja launches, add it to a “free this week” Arena rotation that grants play access only.
    3. Borrow Yiren Zhi Xia's separate PvE and PvP skill kits, so a new ninja's Arena strength and Ranked Points strength can be tuned independently.
